About Ghostly Tools 👻

Ghostly Tools is a professional suite for online exploration and dark web utilities. Here's a breakdown:

  • IP Pinger: Verifies online servers and device status.
  • IP Lookup: Provides detailed info on IPs, location, and ISP.
  • Savage Multitool: A dark web multi-tool connecting through Pinger, PuTTY, and Onion Tour Browser for safe access to markets.
  • Pingers: Monitor servers and websites efficiently.
  • DDoS v3: Controlled network testing for learning and analysis.

All operations prioritize privacy, safety, and ethical usage.
